My Precious Family

"Family — where life begins and love never ends."

I come from a big family of eight siblings, and I’m the youngest among all of them. Being the last child, I guess it’s natural that I turned out a little spoiled in a cute way, of course. Honestly, I’ve always felt that my parents shower me with extra love and attention, and it’s something I quietly cherish, hehe.Growing up, my family has been my strongest support system. My parents are the ones who guide me, motivate me, and remind me to stay strong whenever I feel lost. My siblings, despite having their own lives and responsibilities, have always been there to help me, give advice, and make me feel included. I’m truly grateful to be born into a family filled with warmth, support, and genuine affection.

My Little Niece and Nephew

I have ten nieces and nephews, and they are all very cute with their own unique personalities. Sometimes they can be a handful, but they are the ones who bring the most joy to our family. The house always feels livelier when they are around. Every time they call me ‘Ciksu’, I feel closer to them, and it makes me happy to see them growing up in front of my eyes.
